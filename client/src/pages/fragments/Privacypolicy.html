Privacy Policy

At AspireEdge Private Limited, accessible from https://www.aspireedge.com, the privacy of our users is a top priority. This Privacy Policy explains what kind of personal information we collect, how we use it, and how we protect it.

If you have any questions or concerns about this policy, please contact us directly.

This Privacy Policy applies only to information collected through our website and not to any data collected offline or through other channels.

1. Consent

By using our website, you agree to this Privacy Policy and consent to our collection and use of your personal data as described here.

2. Information We Collect

We collect personal information only when you voluntarily provide it to us. This may include:

  • Your name, email address, phone number, and company name
  • Your address and contact details during account registration
  • Messages or attachments you send when contacting us directly

We make it clear why we are collecting your information at the point it is requested. Your information will only be used by AspireEdge Private Limited and will not be shared with third parties without your consent.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • Operating and maintaining our website
  • Improving our content and user experience
  • Understanding how visitors use our site
  • Responding to inquiries and providing customer support
  • Sending updates, promotional content, and other communication
  • Detecting and preventing fraud or misuse

4. Log Files

Like many websites, we use log files to collect basic data about website visits. These include:

  • IP address
  • Browser type
  • Internet service provider (ISP)
  • Time and date of visit
  • Pages visited
  • Click patterns

This information is not linked to personally identifiable data and is used for analyzing trends, administering the site, and gathering demographics.

5. Cookies and Web Beacons

Our website uses cookies to personalize your experience. Cookies help us remember user preferences and provide tailored content based on your browser and behavior.

Third-party services (like advertising partners) may also use cookies, web beacons, or JavaScript for measuring ad effectiveness or customizing content.

You can disable cookies via your browser settings. For more on cookies, visit: All About Cookies

6. Advertising Partners

We may partner with third-party advertisers who use cookies and other technologies in their ads. These partners automatically receive your IP address when their ads are shown on our site.

AspireEdge Private Limited does not control or access the cookies or tracking methods used by these third-party advertisers. We recommend reviewing their individual privacy policies for more information.

7. Third-Party Privacy Policies

Our Privacy Policy does not apply to other websites or advertisers. We encourage you to check the privacy policies of any third-party websites you visit through links on our site.

You can also manage or disable cookies through your browser settings.

8. CCPA Privacy Rights (For California Users)

Under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), users from California have the right to:

  • Request access to the personal data we’ve collected about you
  • Request that we delete your personal data
  • Opt out of the sale of your personal data (Note: We do not sell your data)

If you wish to exercise any of these rights, please contact us. We will respond within one month.

9. GDPR Data Protection Rights (For European Users)

If you are located in the EU or EEA, you have the following rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR):

  • Access – Request a copy of your personal data
  • Rectification – Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Erasure – Request deletion of your data under certain conditions
  • Restriction – Request limited use of your data
  • Objection – Object to our processing of your data
  • Data Portability – Request transfer of your data to another organization

To exercise any of these rights, contact us directly. We will respond within one month.

10. Children’s Privacy

We are committed to protecting children online. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13 years of age.

If you believe your child has provided us with personal data, please contact us immediately. We will promptly remove such information from our records.
