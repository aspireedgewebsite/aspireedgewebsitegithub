What We Teach

Hybrid Electric Vehicles In Practice

A Hybrid Electric Vehicle (HEV) combines an internal combustion engine with an electric propulsion system to optimize performance, fuel economy, and reduce emissions.

Dual Power Systems

HEVs operate using both fuel engines and electric motors, offering a balanced approach to performance and sustainability.

Fuel Efficiency Boost

Electric motors assist during acceleration and low-speed travel, improving mileage and lowering emissions.

Types of HEVs

Learn about parallel, series, and plug-in hybrids and how each contributes differently to vehicle performance.
